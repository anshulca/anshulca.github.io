Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

Your practice stays yours

Naam Jap is built around a simple principle: your jap counts and sadhana are private. No account is required, and your data is not stored on any server.

Where your data lives

Your practice data (counters, malas, naam written, streaks, settings) is kept locally in your own browser using standard device storage. It does not leave your device.

What we collect

We do not collect names, contact details or payment information. It is possible that standard web-hosting logs record basic, non-personal technical details (such as the page requested) as is routine for any website.

Clearing your data

You can clear your practice data at any time from within the app or by clearing your browser's site data.

Third parties & analytics

We do not run third-party advertising or behavioural trackers on this site.
