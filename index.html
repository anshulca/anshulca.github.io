The root

Why 108?

A mala holds 108 beads, and one mala is one full round of jap. 108 returns again and again in the Sanskrit tradition - the 9 planets across 12 zodiac signs, the measure of a single day, and the distance from the heart to the Name. It is a number for a full, self-contained moment of practice.
