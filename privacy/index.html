Core Principle: Dhun: Naam Jap & Sadhana is built with an offline-first, zero-telemetry architecture. Your sacred chants, malas, Naam Lekhan notebook entries, and sadhana goals remain 100% on your personal device. We do not collect, transmit, store, sell, or share any personal data with any server, third-party entity, or advertiser.

॥ 1. Scope & Overview

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile application Dhun: Naam Jap & Sadhana (Package Name: com.dhun.jap) and its web companion hosted at https://jap.studyfromnotes.com/, developed and published by CA Anshul Karwa.

Spiritual sadhana is deeply personal and sacred. This application is intentionally engineered to require no accounts, no sign-in, and no profile creation. You can perform your daily chanting in absolute peace, without surveillance, behavioral tracking, or algorithmic profiling.

॥ 2. Information We Do NOT Collect

In accordance with Google Play's User Data transparency requirements, we unequivocally declare that our application does not collect or process:

  • Personal Identifiable Information (PII): No names, phone numbers, email addresses, dates of birth, or identification documents are requested or gathered.
  • Location Data: No access to precise GPS location or coarse network location.
  • Device Media or Files: No access to your photo gallery, file documents, camera, or external storage outside the application's isolated sandbox.
  • Contact Lists: No access to or uploading of your device contacts.
  • Financial & Payment Data: No collection of credit/debit card details, bank information, or UPI credentials.
  • Advertising Identifiers: No logging or tracking of Google Advertising ID (AAID) or IDFA for advertising or analytical categorization.

॥ 3. How Practice Data is Handled (Local Device Storage)

All data generated during your devotional practice is stored strictly and exclusively on your local device using standard HTML5 localStorage within the secure Android application sandbox:

  • Naam Jap Tallies: Count numbers, completed malas (108 beads), and chosen names (e.g., Shri Ram, Krishna, Shiva, Hanuman, etc.).
  • Naam Lekhan Notebook: Digital notebook pages and completed mantra writings.
  • Daily Sadhana Goals: Personal sadhana targets, daily logs, and consecutive day streaks.
  • User Preferences: Haptic vibration toggles, sound preferences, recitation speed, and light/dark theme selection.

Offline-First Guarantee: This data never leaves your device and is never uploaded to any cloud backend or external server.

॥ 4. Device Permissions & Hardware Usage

The application requests only the minimum native Android operating system permissions necessary to provide user-requested features:

  • Vibration (android.permission.VIBRATE): Enables tactile haptic feedback when counting mala beads and a distinct vibration notification upon completing a full 108-bead mala. Haptic feedback can be disabled at any time in the app settings.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Used solely by the Android WebView container for standard runtime rendering. No personal data, tracking packets, or analytical payloads are transmitted over the network.
  • Audio Playback & Text-to-Speech: The app utilizes the native Android SoundPool engine to play bundled local audio files and the device's native on-device TextToSpeech service for holy name and stotra pronunciation. No audio is ever recorded, listened to, or transmitted.

॥ 5. Third-Party Sharing, Advertising & Tracking

We do not participate in commercial data monetization:

  • Zero Data Sharing: We do not sell, rent, lease, trade, transfer, or disclose user data to third parties, data brokers, or marketing networks.
  • No Analytics or Tracking SDKs: The application does not bundle third-party tracking libraries, analytics trackers, or session recording SDKs.
  • No Targeted Advertisements: The app does not serve personalized or behavioral advertisements.

॥ 6. User Data Control & Deletion Rights

Under Google Play's User Data and Data Deletion policies, users retain absolute ownership and control over their data:

  • In-App Reset: You can reset or clear individual counters, notebooks, or daily goals directly through the interactive controls within the application.
  • System-Level Deletion: You can purge all stored app data at any time via Android system settings:
    Settings → Apps → Dhun: Naam Jap & Sadhana → Storage → Clear Storage / Clear Data.
  • Complete Removal Upon Uninstall: Uninstalling the application automatically, immediately, and permanently deletes all locally stored data from your device.

॥ 7. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy)

Dhun is a spiritual utility suitable for users of all ages. We strictly adhere to the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families Policy. Because the application does not collect personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect, store, or share personal information from children under 13 (or under 16 in relevant jurisdictions).

॥ 8. Data Security Safeguards

Because practice data remains local to your device, it is protected by the sandboxing architecture of the Android Operating System, preventing other apps from accessing your private counts. There is zero risk of cloud server breaches, third-party database leaks, or network interception.

॥ 9. Updates to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect platform enhancements or statutory guidelines. Any updates will be posted immediately at https://jap.studyfromnotes.com/privacy/ with an updated revision date.
